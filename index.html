Young Omani Engineer Builds Software for the Nation’s Hospitals

From Flutter apps to OutSystems health systems and an offline AI chatbot, Muhannad Al Shammakhi is turning code into care.

By Muhannad Al Shammakhi · Software Engineer 3 min read

MUSCAT — A Software Engineering graduate of the University of Technology and Applied Sciences (UTAS), Muhannad Al Shammakhi has spent the past year building software where it matters most: inside Oman’s healthcare sector.

Since November 2025 he has worked with the Directorate General of Information Technology & Digital Health, developing and maintaining web applications in OutSystems — integrating APIs, building screens and business logic, and testing digital health systems.

Proficient in Flutter and grounded in software engineering principles, his strengths are application development, system design and problem-solving — and he has added a new skill or certificate almost every year since 2022.

“Passionate about developing high-quality software solutions and contributing to collaborative teams.”

Quran Hayati Keeps Memorizers on Track

A full-stack Arabic platform built and deployed end to end.

Right-to-left web app for tracking Quran memorization: weekly learning plans, week-over-week comparisons, monthly PDF reports, three user roles, Excel bulk sign-up, WhatsApp reminders and phone-number login — with data protected by PostgreSQL Row Level Security.

Filed under: PostgreSQL RLS, Arabic RTL, PDF reports

Offline AI Chatbot Answers HR Questions

Built for Tamimah Telecom — and it works without the internet.

A bilingual English/Arabic assistant that answers employee policy questions using RAG, hybrid TF-IDF + BM25 search, fuzzy matching, a pre-built knowledge base and a local AI model, behind a web chat interface.

Filed under: Python, Flask, RAG, Local AI
